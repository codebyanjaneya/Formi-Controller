# Decisions log

Running log of every design decision. One entry per decision, newest at the bottom.

Entry format:
- **ID**: D001, D002, ...
- **Date**
- **Step**
- **DECISION**
- **WHY** we chose it
- **HOW** it works (short)
- **ALTERNATIVES** rejected and why not
- **TRADE-OFF** / what it costs
- **What would change our mind**
- **Files affected**
